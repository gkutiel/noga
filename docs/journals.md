# Candidate journals

The paper combines demand forecasting, price forecasting and decision-focused (cost-oriented) learning, and it reports savings in money. It also contributes an open dataset. The table runs from the best fit to the weakest.

| Journal | Publisher | Why it fits | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Applied Energy**](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/applied-energy) | Elsevier | Publishes applied forecasting with economic impact. Cites like ours: Wang et al. 2019 (pinball LSTM), Li et al. 2024 (joint load–price). | High volume, so reviewers expect a clear novelty claim. Ours is clear. |
| [**International Journal of Forecasting**](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-journal-of-forecasting) | Elsevier (IIF) | The top forecasting venue. Value-oriented and cost-based evaluation is in scope (Hong et al., Weron). | Expects strong statistical rigour. Reviewers may ask for more seeds, several price draws and probabilistic baselines. |
| [**IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid**](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/RecentIssue.jsp?punumber=5165411) | IEEE | Load forecasting with a market or operational objective. The decision-focused papers we cite are often here. | Strict length limit (two-column). The paper would need to be shortened further. |
| [**IEEE Transactions on Power Systems**](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/RecentIssue.jsp?punumber=59) | IEEE | Market and operations audience. The newsvendor procurement framing fits. | Reviewers may question the synthetic imbalance prices more than elsewhere. |
| [**Electric Power Systems Research**](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/electric-power-systems-research) | Elsevier | Published Zhang, Wang & Hug 2022 (cost-oriented load forecasting), our closest cost-oriented reference. | Lower profile than the venues above. |
| [**International Journal of Electrical Power & Energy Systems**](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-journal-of-electrical-power-and-energy-systems) | Elsevier | Load and price forecasting for market participants. Gold open access. | Article processing charge (APC). |
| [**Energy and AI**](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/energy-and-ai) | Elsevier | A small, cheap neural model with a clear energy application. Open access. | APC. The audience is more ML than markets. |
| [**Energy Economics**](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/energy-economics) | Elsevier | Fits if the savings estimate and the market argument are put first. | Expects economic modelling and literature over ML detail. Would need reframing. |
| [**Data in Brief**](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/data-in-brief) (companion paper) | Elsevier | A short data descriptor for the Israeli market dataset, citable on its own. | Not a venue for the main paper; submit it alongside. |

**Recommendation:**
- Submit first to *Applied Energy*, which has the best fit of scope, audience and length.
- Fall back to *Electric Power Systems Research* or *IJEPES*.
- If we add the several-price-draw robustness check and a probabilistic baseline, *International Journal of Forecasting* becomes realistic.

Before submitting, check each journal's current scope, length limits and APC.
